# Free-First External Validation Package

This package groups the no-cost preparation work for the remaining external production-readiness gates.

Included documents:

- `FREE_FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` — overall strategy and evidence-state rules;
- `LGPD_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md` — pre-review data-processing matrix without claiming legal approval;
- `PROVIDER_HOMOLOGATION_PACKAGE.md` — provider contractual/compliance questionnaire and sandbox checks;
- `INTERNAL_SECURITY_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md` — free internal self-assessment checklist;
- `FREE_REVIEW_OUTREACH_TEMPLATE.md` — non-spam outreach templates for pro-bono/community review.

These documents do not convert independent pentest, legal/LGPD review or provider homologation to PASS. They reduce the amount of paid/external work by ensuring that evidence, questions and scope are prepared before any third party becomes involved.

Production/real-money activation remains `NO-GO` until the mandatory independent/external decisions in `PRODUCTION_READINESS_EVIDENCE.md` are actually resolved.
